1. The 3 Pillars of Observability in HydroPond AI

OpenTelemetry standardizes observability collection across distributed services into three correlated pillars:

  • Distributed Tracing: Ingress HTTP requests are captured by FlaskInstrumentor. Within the request, discrete child spans isolate latencies across contour parsing, bicubic DEM generation, D8 flow routing, river buffer filtering, reverse-BFS catchment delineate, and Pareto ranking.
  • Runtime Metrics: PrometheusMetricReader converts OpenTelemetry counter and histogram instruments into standard Prometheus exposition format served at /metrics.
  • Log Correlation: TraceContextLogFilter dynamically queries active span contexts to inject trace_id and span_id into all log records, linking logs directly to traces.

2. Computational Latency Breakdown

OpenTelemetry tracing eliminates blind spots by capturing execution latency for each stage of the AI pipeline:

  • kml_parser.parse_contours: Extracts 3D contour vertices from uploaded KML/KMZ files.
  • dem_builder.build_dem: Bicubic raster interpolation and Gaussian smoothing.
  • terrain_analysis.flow_routing: D8 steepest-descent flow direction, flow accumulation, river detection, and exclusion zone buffering.
  • catchment.find_pond_candidates: Reverse-BFS graph traversal delineating upstream drainage basins.
  • pond_selector.score_and_select: Multi-criteria weighted scoring, Open-Meteo historical rainfall querying, and Pareto selection.
  • geojson_builder.build_geojson: Serialization into spatial GeoJSON for Leaflet map overlay.
